Three locations, one chart, one doctor who already knows the case

Walk in visits

Two windows every open day, 9:00 to 11:00 and 2:00 to 4:00. No appointment needed. You are seen in the order you arrive, with true emergencies triaged first.

Same day sick visits

Room is held in the daily schedule for pets who got sick this morning. Call before 11:00 and you will usually be seen the same day, and if the case belongs at an emergency hospital we will say so.

Preventive care and diagnostics

Exams, vaccinations, and heartworm testing, with digital radiology, ultrasound, and an in house laboratory. Bloodwork runs in the building, so most answers come back during the visit.

Multi pet appointments

Book two, three, or four pets into one block instead of separate slots on separate days. The room is held long enough that nobody gets rushed and you make one trip.

Drop off visits

Leave your pet in the morning and collect them after work. Useful for nervous animals who settle better without an audience, and for owners who cannot sit in a waiting room mid shift.

Phovia light and laser therapy

Wellness and preventive care
